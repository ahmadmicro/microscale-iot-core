What this service does

One pipe from the equipment in your building to a page you can read.

⚡

Live monitoring

Load, voltage, tank levels and machine state, updating as they change — with grid ⇄ generator clearly marked so you know when you're burning fuel.

🎛️

Control

Switch equipment from the dashboard. Every command is confirmed by the device itself, so what you see is the real state, not a hopeful guess.

📈

History & carbon

Trends over time, plus energy split by source — the basis for Scope 1 and Scope 2 reporting rather than an estimate after the fact.

What to do next

Dashboards here are private — each one opens with its own access link.

  1. You were sent a link Open it, then add it to your home screen — it installs like an app and keeps working. Your browser remembers the key, so the link only has to be used once. New to it? Read the user guide.
  2. You're setting up a site Commission the gateway and devices, then ask your administrator to onboard the site — that issues the gateway credential and the dashboard links in one step.
  3. You're checking whether the service is up The health endpoint is public: /api/health. Everything else — readings, history and dashboards — requires a token.
  4. You'd like monitoring for your site Get in touch with Microscale and we'll scope the devices and the install.
