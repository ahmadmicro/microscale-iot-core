# Using your dashboard

*For anyone monitoring a site. No technical knowledge needed.*

Your installer gave you a link. Open it in the browser on your phone or computer — that's the
whole setup. Everything below explains what you're looking at.

## Put it on your home screen

The dashboard is an app that lives at a link (no app store). To make it feel like an app:

- **iPhone (Safari):** tap Share → **Add to Home Screen**.
- **Android (Chrome):** tap the ⋮ menu → **Add to Home screen** (or "Install app").

It opens full-screen from its own icon, and it still opens during internet outages — showing the
last values it saw.

## Reading the screen

- **The pill in the top-right corner** is your connection:
  **live** (green) — you're seeing real-time data; **reconnecting… / offline** — the values on
  screen are the last known ones, not current.
- **Tabs** under the title switch between pages (e.g. *Overview*, *History*).
- **Number cards** show a live value — power, tank level, temperature. A small line under a value
  is its recent trend.
- **Gauge cards** (like Voltage) show where a value sits in its healthy range. The bar turns
  **amber** when the value is outside the marked comfort band.
- **Power source** shows where your electricity is coming from *right now*: a blue **grid** badge,
  or an orange **generator** badge. If it says generator, you're burning fuel.
- **Switch cards** control real equipment. Tap the toggle: it dims for a moment, then flips when
  the device itself confirms. If it snaps back, the device didn't accept the command — try again
  or contact your installer.
- **History pages** chart the last 24 hours: the line is the average, the shaded band shows the
  swing between lowest and highest.

## Colors that ask for attention

- **A red ring around a card** — that value has crossed its alert threshold (for example, the
  diesel tank below 15%). It clears by itself when the value recovers.
- **An amber value** — the reading is *stale*: it was recorded during a connection outage and
  replayed afterwards, or the sensor hasn't reported recently. The number is real, just not live.

## Getting notified

Tap the **bell** in the top bar and allow notifications. While the dashboard is open (including
minimized on most phones), an alert crossing its threshold pops up a notification. One
notification per event — it won't nag you every second.

## If your link says "viewer"

Some links open the dashboard in view-only mode: you'll see fewer pages and the switches can't be
tapped. That's intentional — ask whoever runs your site for an operator link if you need control.

## When something looks wrong

1. Check the connection pill. **offline** means your phone can't reach the site — the site itself
   is usually fine.
2. One card frozen while others update usually means that one sensor is offline — its battery, or
   its wiring. Note which card and tell your installer.
3. Everything frozen with a green **live** pill is worth reporting too — that's the site's gateway
   box not sending.
